	1. Instant Pot Beef Pot Roast
A hearty pressure-cooker meal featuring tender beef chuck that pulls apart easily, simmered alongside vegetables in an enriched stock base.
Ingredients
* 1.2 to 1.5 kg beef chuck roast (cut into 2 to 3 large chunks)
* 1 tbsp olive oil
* 1 brown onion, quartered
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 500g baby potatoes, halved
* 3 large carrots, cut into chunky batons
* 500ml beef stock
* 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
* 1 tbsp tomato paste
* 2 sprigs fresh rosemary (or 1 tsp dried)
* 3 sprigs fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
* 1 bay leaf
* 2 tbsp cornflour mixed with 2 tbsp cold water (for gravy)
* Salt and freshly cracked black pepper
Method
1. Season the beef generously on all sides with salt and black pepper.
2. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté (High). Add the olive oil and brown the beef chunks deeply on all sides (3 to 4 minutes per side), then transfer to a plate.
3. Add the quartered onion and garlic to the pot, sautéing for 1 minute. Stir in tomato paste, Worcestershire sauce, and beef stock. Use a wooden spoon to thoroughly scrape up every brown bit from the bottom of the pot to avoid a burn notice.
4. Return the beef and resting juices to the pot. Nestle the baby potatoes, carrots, rosemary, thyme, and bay leaf around and over the meat.
5. Lock the lid, seal the steam valve, and select Pressure Cook (High) for 45 minutes.
6. Allow a 15-minute Natural Release (NR), then vent any remaining pressure manually.
7. Remove the beef and vegetables to a warm serving platter and shred the beef into generous chunks.
8. Switch the pot back to Sauté mode, whisk in the cornflour slurry, and bubble for 2 to 3 minutes until the sauce thickens into rich gravy. Pour over the beef and serve.
2. Instant Pot Chicken Noodle Soup
Comforting homemade chicken soup cooked rapidly under pressure, finished with fresh lemon and egg noodles.
Ingredients
* 500g boneless, skinless chicken breasts or thighs
* 1 tbsp olive oil or butter
* 1 brown onion, diced
* 2 stalks celery, sliced
* 2 medium carrots, sliced into rounds
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1.5L chicken stock
* 1 tsp dried oregano or thyme
* 1 bay leaf
* 150g dried egg noodles (or spiral pasta)
* Handful fresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
* Juice of 1/2 lemon
* Salt and black pepper to taste
Method
1. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté. Heat the oil or butter, add the diced onion, celery, and carrots, and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until softened. Add the minced garlic and dried herbs, stirring for 30 seconds.
2. Pour in the chicken stock and deglaze the base of the pot. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Lower the raw chicken pieces into the broth and submerge the bay leaf.
4. Lock the lid and cook on Pressure Cook (High) for 8 minutes (breasts) or 10 minutes (thighs).
5. Perform a Quick Release (QR). Carefully remove the chicken to a chopping board and shred with two forks.
6. Turn on Sauté mode, drop the dried egg noodles directly into the bubbling broth, and simmer for 5 to 6 minutes until tender.
7. Turn off the heat. Return the shredded chicken to the pot and stir through the chopped parsley and fresh lemon juice.
3. Hearty Lentil Soup
A nutrient-packed, wholesome soup built on brown lentils, warm aromatics, tomatoes, and tender greens.
Ingredients
* 1.5 cups (approx. 300g) dried brown or green lentils, rinsed and picked over
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 brown onion, finely chopped
* 2 medium carrots, diced
* 2 stalks celery, diced
* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tsp ground cumin
* 1 tsp smoked paprika
* 1 can (400g) crushed or diced tomatoes
* 1.25L vegetable or chicken stock
* 1 bay leaf
* 2 packed cups baby spinach or shredded kale
* Juice of 1/2 lemon
* Salt and cracked black pepper
Method
1. Set the Instant Pot to Sauté. Heat the olive oil and cook the onion, carrots, and celery for 4 minutes until softened.
2. Add the minced garlic, ground cumin, and smoked paprika, stirring constantly for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and stock, scraping up any caramelisation from the base.
4. Stir in the rinsed lentils and bay leaf.
5. Lock the lid and set to Pressure Cook (High) for 15 minutes.
6. Allow a 10-minute Natural Release, then vent any remaining pressure.
7. Discard the bay leaf. Fold in the fresh greens and lemon juice, letting residual heat wilt the greens for about 2 minutes. Season to taste with salt and black pepper.
4. Banana Pancakes
Quick and naturally sweet pancakes perfect for using ripe bananas on weekend mornings.
Ingredients
* 2 medium very ripe bananas, thoroughly mashed
* 2 large eggs
* 1 cup (150g) plain flour (or self-raising flour)
* 1 tsp baking powder (omit if using self-raising flour)
* 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
* 1/4 cup (60ml) milk
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* Pinch of salt
* Butter or neutral oil for frying
Method
1. In a mixing bowl, mash bananas thoroughly until smooth. Whisk in eggs, milk, and vanilla extract until well combined.
2. In a separate small bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt. Fold into the wet ingredients just until combined (do not overmix; small lumps are fine).
3. Heat a non-stick frying pan or skillet over medium-low heat and melt a knob of butter.
4. Pour 2 to 3 tablespoons of batter per pancake into the pan.
5. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until bubbles appear on top and edges appear set, then gently flip and cook for another 1 to 2 minutes until golden.
6. Serve warm with maple syrup, sliced fresh fruit, or yoghurt.
5. Banana Waffles
Crispy on the outside and fluffy inside with natural sweetness and aromatic cinnamon.
Ingredients
* 1 large very ripe banana, mashed (approx. 1/2 cup)
* 1.5 cups (225g) plain flour
* 2 tsp baking powder
* 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
* 2 tbsp brown sugar
* Pinch of salt
* 2 large eggs (separated: yolks in one bowl, whites in another)
* 1 cup (250ml) milk
* 1/4 cup (60g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
Method
1. Preheat your waffle iron according to the manufacturer instructions.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the plain flour, baking powder, cinnamon, brown sugar, and salt.
3. In a medium bowl, combine mashed banana, egg yolks, milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
4. In a separate clean bowl, beat the egg whites to soft peaks using a hand whisk or electric beaters.
5. Stir the banana mixture into the dry ingredients until just combined. Gently fold in the whipped egg whites in two batches using a spatula.
6. Lightly grease the waffle iron. Ladle in batter and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until deep golden and crisp.
6. Classic Chewy Oatmeal Cookies
Buttery, cinnamon-spiced oatmeal cookies with lightly crisp edges and soft, chewy centres.
Ingredients
* 1.5 cups (135g) traditional rolled oats
* 1 cup (150g) plain flour
* 1/2 tsp baking soda
* 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 115g unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
* 1/2 cup (100g) packed brown sugar
* 1/4 cup (50g) caster sugar
* 1 large egg, room temperature
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* Optional: 3/4 cup sultanas, raisins, or dark chocolate chips
Method
1. Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line two baking trays with baking paper.
2. In a bowl, whisk together the plain flour, rolled oats, baking soda, cinnamon, and salt.
3. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and caster sugar with an electric mixer until creamy (about 2 minutes).
4. Beat in the egg and vanilla extract until completely incorporated.
5. Add dry ingredients to the butter mixture, stirring with a spatula until a coherent dough forms. Fold in sultanas or choc chips if using.
6. Scoop 1.5-tablespoon balls of dough onto the prepared trays, leaving 5cm spacing. Lightly flatten each ball with fingers.
7. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until edges are golden and centres remain slightly soft.
8. Allow cookies to cool on the tray for 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to firm up completely.
7. Simple Homemade Flatbread (No Yeast Wrap)
A pliable four-ingredient flatbread that rolls thin and stays soft for wraps, burritos, or dipping.
Ingredients
* 2 cups (300g) plain flour (plus extra for dusting)
* 1/2 tsp fine salt
* 3/4 cup (180ml) warm water
* 2 tbsp olive oil (or melted butter)
Method
1. In a large bowl, whisk the flour and salt together. Create a well in the centre.
2. Pour warm water and olive oil into the well. Stir with a wooden spoon until a shaggy dough forms.
3. Tip onto a lightly floured surface and knead for 3 to 4 minutes until smooth and elastic.
4. Cover dough with a damp tea towel and rest for 15 to 20 minutes to relax the gluten.
5. Divide into 6 equal portions. Roll each piece on a floured surface into a thin circle (approx. 2mm thick, 20cm diameter).
6. Heat a dry cast-iron skillet or heavy frying pan over medium-high heat.
7. Place one flatbread into the dry pan. Cook for 60 to 90 seconds until large bubbles inflate and golden spots appear underneath.
8. Flip and cook for 45 to 60 seconds on the second side.
9. Stack cooked flatbreads wrapped inside a clean tea towel to keep warm and pliable.
8. 2-Ingredient Greek Yoghurt Flatbread
A quick stovetop flatbread with no resting time required, yielding a pillowy, naan-like crumb.
Ingredients
* 1 cup (150g) self-raising flour (or 1 cup plain flour + 1.5 tsp baking powder + 1/4 tsp salt)
* 1 cup (240g) plain Greek yoghurt (thick whole-milk style)
* Extra flour for dusting and rolling
* Optional: 1 tbsp melted garlic butter and chopped parsley for brushing
Method
1. In a bowl, combine the self-raising flour and Greek yoghurt.
2. Stir with a spatula until dough starts clumping together.
3. Tip onto a generously floured bench and knead for 1 to 2 minutes into a smooth ball (add light dustings of flour if sticky).
4. Divide dough into 4 portions (for thicker naan bread) or 6 portions (for thinner wraps).
5. Roll each portion out with a rolling pin to 4mm to 5mm thickness.
6. Heat a heavy skillet over medium-high heat with a light wipe of oil or dry.
7. Cook flatbread for 2 to 3 minutes until puffed with charred spots underneath, then flip and cook for another 2 minutes until cooked through.
8. Brush with melted garlic butter and herbs immediately if desired.
